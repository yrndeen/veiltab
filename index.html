VeilTab

Midnight Network Buildathon — Wave 3 (AKINDO). A privacy-first group-tab settlement dApp built with Compact 0.36.1.

  • A host opens a group tab with a public total and a bounded participant count (1–4).
  • Each participant commits a salted hash of their private share to the public ledger.
  • The sealAndProve circuit proves in zero knowledge that private shares match their commitments and sum exactly to the public total, moving the tab to Balanced without revealing individual shares on-chain.
  • Any participant may later call discloseMyShare to selectively reveal only their own share.
